# LegacyManager

An addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever** that makes the Legacy system easier to use.

> Work in progress – stage 1 of the planned feature set.

## Stage 1 (this version)

- **Clear point summary** in the Legacy tree: points spent against the seasonal limit, and how many earned points only count for the Progress Track.
- **Mark all seen** button in the Legacy challenges, which clears the blue glow of every new challenge at once.
- **Reward tracker**: points missing to the next Progress Track reward, and a reminder when a reward is ready to collect at Innkeeper Wiley in Ratchet.
- **Character data** for the upcoming alt overview: level, XP and rested XP, professions, Hardcore ruleset and Legacy ranks of every character you log in.

## Planned

- Alt overview and copilot with the quickest next Legacy points
- Loadouts and one-click swap at the class trainer, with a warning before unlearning *Talented* below level 60
- Warning when you join a raid without your raid loadout
- Group view of the Legacy perks of party, raid and battleground members

## Commands

`/lm` opens the Legacy window · `/lm seen` · `/lm status` · `/lm minimap` · `/lm errors`
